# Take the lesson, refuse the catalog

Chaduvuko’s homepage should not borrow a section from W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, Tpoint Tech, or roadmap.sh. On 9 October 2026 each of those front doors, logged out, asked a new visitor to sign in, open a paid course, search a directory, or scan title cards, and none of them put a written lesson on the first screen. The objects those pages scatter — a specimen of the work, a job with one sentence, a track with a count, a practice link that does not want an account — are already this page, in that order, and a returning browser already gets “Pick up where you left off” from local progress instead of a fake streak. Three controls still behave like the catalogs they came from. **Start with one lesson today** opens the track index, the same place as **See all tracks**. The eight cards under **N tracks, from zero to job-ready** show an area and a lesson count, not the one-line purpose already written for the Tracks menu. **Try it in the browser** paints a Run mark that is not a button. Those are the changes. A 31-role grid, a language wall, a hero search box, an app, a certificate, a Pro pack, and a second sticky bar are the other homepages’ actual ideas, and they stay there.

## None of the four first screens contains a lesson

W3Schools opens on an account. The anonymous hero’s H1 is **Learn to Code**, the line **No sign-up needed, just start learning** is a bold tag rather than a link, and the first body button is a green **Sign in** on a padlocked card of **12,560**, **XP this week 850**, and a **7** day streak. Those numbers are string literals in the hero script, identical for every logged-out browser, and the carousel rotates through Spaces, leagues, and an avatar ([W3Schools](https://www.w3schools.com/), [dashboard-lite.js](https://www.w3schools.com/lib/account/dashboard-lite.js?updated=20260902)). The tutorial block, **What do you want to learn?**, starts at about y=2111 on a 390px screen, after an Adventure app band that is **1051px** tall at 390 and 320. There is no lesson title and no paragraph of instruction. The HTML “example” is a styled div, and **Try it Yourself »** leaves for a try-it page in a new tab. The Python chip swaps in two lines, `if 5 > 2:` and a print, and then offers **Python Certificate** at the same size as **Learn Python** ([W3Schools](https://www.w3schools.com/)).

GeeksforGeeks has no H1. The first body text is **Start Your Learning Today!**, and the first links are **Placement 360**, **Data Science 360**, and **Full Stack Dev**, the filled chip opening a course in a new tab ([GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/), [Placement 360](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/courses/placement-360-cip-complete-tech-interview)). Under an **Explore** heading the first screen is category tiles, not articles. At 1440 the visible cards run through Java; at 320 the first title is clamped to “Data Structure and Alg...” and the chip row clips its own ends. The Python tile is a hub whose H1 is “Python Tutorial,” and the homepage stylesheet still describes a search field the hydrated page does not render ([Python Tutorial](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/python/python-programming-language-tutorial/)).

Tpoint Tech’s H1 is **Welcome to Tpoint Tech**. At 1440 the first screen is that heading, a “500+ tutorials” sentence, a **Search...** field, a stock photo badged **500+ TUTORIALS**, and the start of an H3 “Python,” with the first name card five pixels below a 900px viewport ([Tpoint Tech](https://www.tpointtech.com/)). The sentence **Start learning programming language of your choice** is in an HTML comment. The card that does appear links to a hub, H1 “Python Tutorial,” not to the chapter `what-is-python` ([Python tutorial](https://www.tpointtech.com/python-tutorial)). At 390 the photo is gone and four name cards fit; at 320 the first screen reaches the top of one card. The only chapter titles on the page are 29 Java links under **Latest Update**, near the bottom of a page that is about 10,000px at 1440 and about 40,000px at 320.

roadmap.sh’s H1 is **Developer Roadmaps**, and the first control is a search field, placeholder **What do you want to learn today?**, with the helper **Search available roadmaps or learn anything with AI** ([roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/)). There is no written lesson in that hero. The next ask is a tablist, then **Role-based** and the count **31**, then a purple **Learn with AI** button that is not one of the 31. Typing “data engineer” in this session left the words in the field, left Frontend and Backend on the page, and Enter stayed at `https://roadmap.sh/`. A physical ⌘K was not shown to open a palette, so a desktop keyboard might still have a shorter path than the one that was observed. It was not observed.

Chaduvuko already inverts that stack, and the inversion is not a proposal. The hero H1 is **Lessons that show how the work really goes.** Beside it, the first-visit card is a live lesson, `/learn/python/io-formatting`, with the track, the module, the title, a code sample, and the **TypeError** that sample produces. **Start learning** goes to `/learn` and **Find your roadmap** goes to `/learn/roadmap`. Under those, the live counts are lessons, tracks, career roadmaps, and projects. When this browser has progress, that same hero is replaced by **Pick up where you left off**, a **Continue lesson** link, and the real lesson title. That is the W3Schools card without the lock and without the invented XP. Do not add a resume module. It is already the hero.

## The filled button opens a login, a course, or a list

The shared failure is the control that looks primary. W3Schools paints **Sign in** at 352×44 and leaves “just start learning” unlinked. At 320 that green button sits at y=857, off a 700px screen, so the first screen is the perk list and the number **12,560** ([W3Schools](https://www.w3schools.com/)). GeeksforGeeks uses the same shape for a product: one filled chip, three course names, no “Start reading” ([GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/)). Tpoint’s first control under the H1 is the search input, 37px tall at every width measured, and a direct request to `/search?q=python` on that date returned **301** to the homepage. The field itself was not typed into, so this is the public URL, not a proof of the in-page script ([Tpoint Tech](https://www.tpointtech.com/)). roadmap.sh’s filled control inside the catalog is **Learn with AI**, sitting in the first cell of the role grid, above Data Engineer at every width, with no lesson behind it on this page ([roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/)).

Chaduvuko’s filled buttons are already the refusal of those four. They do not open a login, a course, or an AI cell. They are also not the lesson. **Start learning** in the hero and **Start learning** under **Start with one lesson today** both go to `/learn`, and **See all N tracks** in the track section goes there too. The only control that opens the lesson the page has already rendered is the card. The hero is allowed to keep that split. The card is the specimen, **Start learning** is the catalog, and **Find your roadmap** is the job path, which is a real choice for someone who does not want the Python I/O lesson. The closing section is not a third path. Its heading promises one lesson, its sentence **No account. Your progress saves in this browser** is already the honest version of W3Schools’ unlinked “no sign-up” line, and its button repeats the track index.

That is the first change, and it is inside **Start with one lesson today**, not a new band. Point that button at the same lesson the hero card opens. Leave the hero’s **Start learning** on `/learn`, and leave **See all N tracks** as the catalog. Do not retarget the hero button at Placement 360’s job, and do not put Tpoint’s **Search...** under the H1. Search already opens from the bar. A second field is what makes Tpoint’s phone a heading plus a box plus a name, and it is what would push the code and the error — the part none of these homepages show — further down a stacked hero. The phone already lays the pitch, both buttons, and the counts above the card. Nothing else goes above it.

## Conclusion

The four homepages are distribution pages that mention reading. This one is a lesson that mentions a catalog, and that order is the whole advantage: the specimen is on the hero, the four jobs are a labeled section rather than item 12 of 31, the tracks are counted and finite, and the practice links do not want an account or an install. The copies worth taking were built when that order was chosen. What the audit still changes is the control that pretends to be the lesson and is not. The last green button should open the lesson the card already shows. The track card should use the sentence the menu already has. The Run mark should become the playground link or disappear. None of that adds a section. The sites that added sections used them to put a login, a course, a directory, or a sticky second bar between the visitor and the page.
