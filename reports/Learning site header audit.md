# Refuse their bars, take the clock

None of W3Schools, GeeksforGeeks, Tpoint Tech, or roadmap.sh has a header Chaduvuko should swap in for the one it already ships. On 9 October 2026 each of them spent the top of a written lesson on a second catalog, a store, or both, and none of them put track, module, lesson position, and the next page into a single bar that stays put. The pieces that actually help a logged-out reader — a blurbed catalog, a marked lesson list, a named next page, search that returns the thing you typed — are already in Chaduvuko’s 64px bar, its lesson picker, its end-of-article cards, or its search dialog. What those lists still show, and the picker still hides, is how long the next lesson takes. That is the only new header input worth building. A language strip, a chip row, a graph that never becomes a lesson, an account wall, and a bar that hides on scroll would make the phone worse.

## Their second rows are catalogs, not the lesson

W3Schools is the pattern people remember, and the memory is the failure. The Python Strings lesson keeps two fixed rows that do not hide after a 900px scroll: a white `#pagetop` bar at **56px** and a black `#subtopnav` strip at **33px**, together **89px** that never leaves ([Python Strings](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_strings.asp)). The strip is about **4,006px** of language names in a 1,440px window, scrollbar hidden, with PYTHON lit and nothing that says Strings or which lesson this is. At 320px it auto-scrolls until the visible chips are a sliced **“ASCRIPT”**, SQL, PYTHON, and a half JAVA, and the page itself is **330px** wide in a 320px window, so Sign In’s right edge sits at **324**. The white bar’s right side, on both the homepage and the lesson, is Sign In, Upgrade, Paid Courses, Spaces, and a NEW Adventure app chip. The phone menu continues the pitch (app, Spaces, Plus, Academy) and does not close on Escape. That strip is the element people would copy onto Chaduvuko. It would become a second row under the one bar, which Chaduvuko does not have and should not grow. The track name is already in the lesson bar from 640px up, and in the article breadcrumb below that. A strip of every language does not say which lesson you are in.

GeeksforGeeks stacks the same mistake with a different second product. The homepage bar is **50px** (logo, search, Courses, Tutorials, Interview Prep, theme, Sign In) plus a **44px** chip row, **95px** stuck together ([homepage](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/)). On the Python article the chip row changes to Python Tutorial, Data Types, Interview Questions, and the rest, and it does not contain “Python Introduction,” Previous, Next, or a lesson number ([Python Introduction](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/python/introduction-to-python/)). Scroll the article and the primary bar leaves while the chip row sticks. At 390 and 320 the top row keeps a **35×35** hamburger with no accessible name and `tabIndex` -1, the logo, and a **32×32** search icon. Escape leaves the drawer open. Chip anchors are about **21px** tall, and the “C” anchor is **8×21**. Courses, written tutorials, and practice are three catalogs, then practice and courses are repeated as chips, and the search-results URL throws the menu out and inserts Jobs. Chaduvuko’s Tracks, Roadmaps, Practice, and Interview Prep are already one catalog with four doors. A second scrolling shelf would not be a fifth door. It would be the GFG chip row.

Tpoint Tech’s green `#link` bar is that shelf again, **46px**, `overflow: hidden`, sticky at `top: 0`, listing Python through OS (Aptitude and Reasoning exist in the homepage HTML and are clipped at 1440) ([Tpoint Tech](https://www.tpointtech.com/), [What is Python](https://www.tpointtech.com/what-is-python)). On the chapter, at 390 and 320, Tutorials, Interviews, and Compilers are gone, the green bar shows a hamburger plus a few language names cut at the edge, and the lesson text is cut mid-word at **x=317** of 320. The hamburger that remains calls `toggleSidebar()`, the chapter list, not the category menus. Interview questions are correctly a different mega-menu from Tutorials. Chaduvuko already ships that split: Interview Prep and Practice are their own header items, and the lesson picker lists only the open track. The green bar is the piece to refuse. It would replace the current track and Next with a sideways list of other tracks.

roadmap.sh does not add a language strip. It deletes the lesson from the bar instead. At 390 and 320 the global bar is the logo, the words **“AI Tutor,”** and a Menu button measured at **16×16** ([Data Engineer](https://roadmap.sh/data-engineer)). The menu is Roadmaps, AI Tutor, Upgrade, Guides, Lesson Packs, and the account links. It does not name Data Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Engineer, or Cyber Security. Desktop is one dark row of Roadmaps, AI Tutor, Packs, Newsletters, Upgrade, Login, and Sign Up, and the hamburger behind the logo is SQL Course, Get Started, Projects, Guides, Teams, and Advertise, not the role list ([roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/)). Chaduvuko’s phone menu already holds Tracks, the four featured roadmaps plus the rest, Practice, and Interview Prep, behind a **44×44** button whose name is Menu. Shrinking that to a 16px icon, or making the only visible word an AI product, is the roadmap.sh phone bar. Do not take it.

## Where you are is already in the one bar

The useful location pattern on these sites is small, and Chaduvuko already uses a stronger version of it. Tpoint’s only reliable “you are here” is the breadcrumb **Home › Python › What is Python**, on screen at 1440, 390, and 320, with no account ([What is Python](https://www.tpointtech.com/what-is-python)). The sidebar does not mark the current row: CSS would paint `a.active` green, and the chapter HTML never adds `active`. W3Schools has no breadcrumb. Location is the lit PYTHON chip, the h1 “Python Strings,” an expanded sidebar group, and Previous/Next, and nothing says which number this lesson is ([Python Strings](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_strings.asp)). GeeksforGeeks has no visible breadcrumb either. The only trail is JSON-LD, and its second item is the slug `introduction-to-python`, not the title. There is no Previous, no Next, and no “N of M.” The Explore row for the article you are reading is `font-weight: 400` on a transparent background ([Python Introduction](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/python/introduction-to-python/)).

Chaduvuko already ships the account-free line those three fail to finish. The lesson bar shows the track from 640px, the module from 1024px, the title from 1200px, and **Lesson N of M** (on a phone, **N of M**). Previous and Next are real links. From 1200px the Next control reads **Next ·** and the neighbor title; below that the visible word is Next, and the accessible name is still “Next lesson” plus the title. The last lesson says Track complete, or Done on a small screen. Under 1024px the article header still has a Home breadcrumb, and from 1024px up that crumb is hidden because the bar already has the track and the module. Adding Tpoint’s crumb as a second row under the bar would repeat a line the bar already owns. Making that crumb sticky would be the second bar these sites use to eat the phone. NN/g’s illustration of a sticky header plus another sticky strip is a content-to-chrome ratio of about **2:1** on a phone, against about **13:1** for a short header ([Sticky Headers](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/sticky-headers/)). W3Schools is already at 89px before the ad. GeeksforGeeks is at 95px on the homepage. Chaduvuko’s 64px bar stays. It does not hide on the way down, which is also the right refusal of the GeeksforGeeks article, where the stable bar scrolls away and the noisy chips remain.

Previous and Next at the end of the article are already shipped too. W3Schools repeats **❮ Previous** and **Next ❯** at both ends of Python Strings, green Next at **45px** tall, no account, hrefs to the neighbor pages, and the visible labels are the words Previous and Next, not the neighbor titles ([Python Strings](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_strings.asp)). Tpoint hides the top pair with `div#bottomnextup { display: none }` and names the following chapter only at the bottom: **Next Topic** and **Python Features** ([What is Python](https://www.tpointtech.com/what-is-python)). Chaduvuko’s end-of-article cards already show the label, the neighbor title, and the section. The wide header already shows the next title. Putting that title into the 320px bar as well would be how W3Schools slices “JAVASCRIPT” into “ASCRIPT.” The phone bar’s job is the word Next. The card’s job is the name. Both exist. Neither is a new idea.

The same is true of the blurbs. W3Schools’ Tutorials panel and `/tutorials/index.php` put one sentence on a topic card (“HTML / The building blocks of every webpage”) and then, in the same panel, a **$95** certificate and the Adventure app ([tutorials index](https://www.w3schools.com/tutorials/index.php)). Chaduvuko’s Tracks menu already has the sentence without the certificate: one line under each track, Foundations marked start here, Data Science marked Early. The Roadmaps menu already names Data Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Engineer, and Cybersecurity Analyst, each with its own line, then All roadmaps. roadmap.sh buries those jobs as four ordinary cards inside a **31-role** grid, under different names (“Machine Learning,” “Cyber Security”), and the phone menu does not list them at all ([roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/)). That grid is not an upgrade to a menu that already features the four jobs.

## A marked list of fourteen beats a rail of 271

The one reading pattern on these sites that is actually about the next lesson is the roadmap.sh Git pack, and Chaduvuko’s lesson picker is already most of it. The pack page is a numbered list: **14 Lessons**, **2.3 hours read**, each row a title, a one-line blurb, and a duration, with FREE on lessons 1 and 4 and a lock on the rest ([Git Fundamentals](https://roadmap.sh/packs/git)). The free lesson throws away the global header and puts the same 14 titles in a left column, the current one marked, with **0 / 14 complete** ([What Is Git?](https://roadmap.sh/packs/git/what-is-git)). Previous Lesson and Next Lesson are real URLs. On a phone that column becomes an “Open lesson list” button, and a separate bottom overlay of Prev, Next, and a yellow AI button sits on top of the prose. The overlay is the second bar. The list is the useful object.

Chaduvuko’s picker is that list, inside the existing bar rather than instead of it. It opens from Lesson N of M, scrolled to the current lesson, grouped by module, numbered, with the current row marked and completed lessons checked. On a phone it is a full-height sheet; on a wide screen it is a bounded popover, not a column of every page in the track. The **0 / 14** counter is not a missing feature. The closed button already says where you are in the sequence, which is the fact a guest needs, and the open list already shows how many are marked done in this browser. roadmap.sh’s counter is drawn for a guest and then the Done button on a roadmap node opens **“You must be logged in to perform this action”** ([Data Engineer](https://roadmap.sh/data-engineer)). Copying Learning / Done / Skip, or a zeroed fraction on the closed bar, would impersonate an account. W3Schools does the same impersonation in public: the anonymous homepage card reads **12,560**, **XP this week 850**, and **Day streak 7**, identical at 1440 and 390, while `localStorage` after a lesson view held a theme and ad ids, not “you were on Python Strings” ([homepage](https://www.w3schools.com/)). Chaduvuko’s 2px line on the bottom edge of the header is scroll position, hidden from assistive tech. It is not a streak, and it should not become one.

What the pack rows have and the picker rows do not is the duration. GeeksforGeeks prints the same fact on Explore and then puts the list in the wrong place: **“1 min read,” “4 min read,”** from `reading_time`, under the article around **y=2249**, current row unmarked ([Python Introduction](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/python/introduction-to-python/)). Chaduvuko already prints a read time under the lesson h1, and on the “what to learn next” suggestions. The sequential cards and the picker omit it. A one-line blurb under every picker title is the wrong half of the pack row. Tpoint’s Python sidebar is **271 links** in **23** groups, every group forced open above 768px, current page unmarked, and the script that was supposed to reopen the current group compares an absolute href to the pathname, so it cannot match ([What is Python](https://www.tpointtech.com/what-is-python)). On a phone that list is a `position: fixed` overlay at z-index 555. A sentence on every row of a long track is that sidebar inside the sheet. The number and the title are enough. The minutes are the missing token.

The left rail Chaduvuko already has on a wide lesson is the in-page table of contents, not a second copy of the track. Replacing it with W3Schools’ `#sidenav` would import a **230px** column whose links are **27px** tall, running under the bars from y=0, and on a phone a second control also named Menu whose close button is commented out and which Escape does not dismiss ([Python Strings](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_strings.asp)). GeeksforGeeks’ desktop lesson is the worse rail: a **792px** column between two **300px** ad columns (`GFG_AD_Leftsidebar_*`, `GFG_AD_Desktop_RightSideBar_*`, including a slot named `BTF_Sticky`), filled in this session with a book ad, plus an in-article “Visit Course” ([Python Introduction](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/python/introduction-to-python/)). At 390 the leaderboard above the W3Schools h1 was **389×55**; at 1440 the same slot was **975×250**. Tpoint’s 390px chapter drew a ZoneId box over the paragraph. The phone lesson on GeeksforGeeks and, under 992px, on W3Schools is full width. Chaduvuko’s lesson is already full width until the wide TOC appears. That is the layout to keep. The TOC stays the TOC. The track stays the picker.

## Search should open the job, not invent one

Header search on these four sites is either a store or a flat list. Chaduvuko’s dialog is already the better tool, with one hole that is not a new control.

W3Schools, logged out, is the part worth recognizing and not rebuilding. Typing “string” into `#tnb-google-search-input` listed String Inspector, Python Strings, Java Strings, and the rest before any login ([Python Strings](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_strings.asp)). The field is real on a desktop. Under 616px it becomes a **36×36** icon. Chaduvuko already opens a dialog from the bar, groups hits into Tracks, Lessons, Roadmaps, Practice, Interview Prep, Articles, and Pages, and puts a context line under the title. On a lesson the trigger is an icon so the bar can hold position and Next; below 389px that icon leaves the bar and a Search row in the menu opens the same dialog. Putting the field back on the 320px lesson bar is how W3Schools gained 10px of sideways scroll. The suggestions-without-an-account behavior is already how the dialog works.

GeeksforGeeks search is one ungrouped Google list. The request hardcodes `type=premium`. A hit whose path is `/problems` or `/explore` gets a **“Problem”** tag, and the CSS hides that word under 600px, so a phone keeps the row and loses the only label that says it is not an article ([gfgsearch API](https://utilapi.geeksforgeeks.org/api/gfgsearch/?page=1&sort=relevance&type=premium&query=binary%20search&search_type=google)). The modal also links to Go Premium. Chaduvuko already separates practice and interview from lessons with group headings that do not disappear on a phone. A “Problem” badge would be a second, weaker version of those headings. The premium flag and the Go Premium link are a refuse.

roadmap.sh’s command-K is a roadmap finder until the first keystroke, and then it competes with itself. The empty state says **“Start typing to find a roadmap, or to create something with AI.”** The query `sql` listed SQL and PostgreSQL, then **“Nothing here yet? Make it,”** then Create a course, Create a roadmap, Create a guide, and Create a quiz ([Git Fundamentals](https://roadmap.sh/packs/git)). Whether packs and guides ever appear as hits was not tested. Chaduvuko’s empty field already offers Continue, recent lessons, and tracks, which is the return path roadmap.sh does not show a guest. Typed results already include roadmaps. The tutor is a separate button under the list, and Enter opens a hit or the results page rather than sending the query to the tutor. Welding “create a quiz” rows into that list is the `sql` panel. Do not take it. The four job names do not need a new empty-state group either. They are already the Roadmaps menu, one click away, which is more than roadmap.sh puts in its phone menu.

Tpoint’s search is the caution against trusting a box that looks anonymous and useful. The UI is a public field, typeahead after two characters, then a modal of ten rows whose sentence is a template: “{title} with examples. Let’s start learning {title}…” A direct request to `/search?q=python` on 9 October 2026 returned **301** to `/` ([search URL](https://www.tpointtech.com/search?q=python)). The field was not typed into, so the dropdown may be dead. Chaduvuko does not need a template sentence under titles it already describes with a track and a section.

## A graph is not the next lesson

roadmap.sh is the site that looks most like Chaduvuko’s job, and its roadmap page is the pattern to refuse. Data Engineer stays at `/data-engineer` when you click Introduction. The panel offers Resources, AI Tutor, Learning, Done, Skip, a short paragraph, two DataCamp courses, one YouTube video, and an ask field. There is no previous or next topic. `/data-engineer/introduction` is a 404 ([Data Engineer](https://roadmap.sh/data-engineer), [missing topic](https://roadmap.sh/data-engineer/introduction)). The first screen is also favorite, “Prefer us on Google,” newsletter, Download, Share, and **“Join 8,048+ others tracking their progress on this roadmap.”** Related boxes send you to other posters (Python for Data Analysis, SQL, AI & Data Scientist, BI Analyst, Data Analyst), not to the next chapter of the job you opened. Done and Add to favorites open a login modal. The login page has no guest path.

Chaduvuko’s roadmap panel is not that drawer. Where a node has a lesson, the panel already links through to it, and progress on the site stays in the browser. The header does not need a Download, a share row, or a sign-up banner to make the roadmap feel like roadmap.sh. The pack is the part of roadmap.sh that sequences reading, and it sequences a skill (Git), not Data Engineer, and then locks lesson 2 behind **“PRO LESSON / Sign in to continue”** while still rendering Previous and Next ([lesson 2](https://roadmap.sh/packs/git/set-up-git-and-create-a-repository)). The link is the lesson. The lock, the AI rail, and the yellow phone button are the store.

Guides are a third object, a single article with an AI rail and, in the first screen, no lesson N of M and no neighbor control ([How to Set Environment Variables in Python](https://roadmap.sh/python/set-environment-variable)). The bottom of that guide was not captured, so a later “next guide” link is unconfirmed. It does not matter for the header. Chaduvuko’s lesson is already a sequenced page, not a one-off article that has to grow a tutor column to say what is next.

## Conclusion

These four headers are distribution systems that happen to have a lesson underneath. W3Schools can keep an 89px stack because the bar’s job is certificates, Spaces, and an app. GeeksforGeeks can keep 95px and a 300px drawer because the bar’s job is courses, practice, and ads. Tpoint can keep a green language strip because the chapter list is allowed to be a 271-link overlay. roadmap.sh can name only “AI Tutor” on a phone because the roadmap is a poster and the account is the progress model. Chaduvuko’s bar already does the job those chrome layers never do: the catalog, the job, the practice, the interview, and, once a lesson is open, the position and the next page. The sites that sequence reading all put the neighbor somewhere the catalog is not — the bottom of the article, or a short list of fourteen — and Chaduvuko already has both. The audit changes one fact inside those existing surfaces, the minutes, and changes nothing about the height, the count of rows, or what happens on scroll.

## Inputs to take

1. On the lesson picker that opens from Lesson N of M, show the read time the catalog already stores and the article already prints under the h1. GeeksforGeeks’ Explore rows and the Git pack’s per-lesson duration are the source ([Python Introduction](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/python/introduction-to-python/), [Git Fundamentals](https://roadmap.sh/packs/git)). Put a short muted time at the end of each existing row. Do not add a rail, a block under the lesson, or a sentence under the title.

2. On the end-of-article Previous and Next cards, show that same read time beside the section line those cards already render. Tpoint’s “Next Topic / Python Features” is the naming Chaduvuko’s cards already do; the clock is the part they skip ([What is Python](https://www.tpointtech.com/what-is-python)). Leave the phone bar’s control as the word Next. The title is already on the card and, from 1200px, in the bar.

## Do not take

- W3Schools’ black `#subtopnav` language strip, the right-hand cluster of Sign In, Upgrade, Paid Courses, Spaces, and the NEW app chip, the homepage XP card (12,560 / 850 / streak 7), and “Get your own Python Server” inside the example. Also refuse a second Menu for the chapter list. Previous, Next, topic blurbs, and logged-out search suggestions are already shipped.
- GeeksforGeeks’ 50px-plus-44px header, the article behavior that hides the primary bar and sticks the chips, the 35×35 nameless drawer, the ad rails and “Visit Course,” Sign In, Go Premium, and the Placement 360 / Data Science 360 / Full Stack Dev chips. Also refuse a “Problem” badge. Group headings already separate practice and interview from lessons.
- Tpoint’s green `#link` shortcut bar, the 271-link sidebar and its fixed phone overlay, the theme dots, and the ad slots that covered the 390px paragraph. Also refuse a second breadcrumb row. The bar and, on small screens, the article crumb already locate the lesson. Do not put interview or practice links inside the lesson picker. That split is already the header.
- roadmap.sh’s Data Engineer graph and Introduction panel, the Login / Sign Up / Upgrade chrome, the “8,048+” banner, the Done and favorite login modal, and the pack’s Pro wall. Also refuse the phone bar that says only “AI Tutor” next to a 16×16 Menu, the lesson’s floating Prev / Next / AI overlay, and command-K rows that create a course, roadmap, guide, or quiz. The four jobs, the numbered picker, and a search dialog that returns real hits are already shipped.
- A second phone bar, a bar that hides on scroll, an account, a paid upsell, a completion fraction or streak on the closed lesson button, and a one-line blurb on every picker row. The 2px line on the header is scroll position. It is not course progress.
