# Enterprise ATS job-posting template (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever)

Research date: 13 October 2026. This note records what those three systems name and what live or indexed public postings show. It does not set a salary, a city, or full-time versus contract for Chaduvuko.

Two layers sit on every serious posting. Structured facts are labeled fields the ATS stores and the board prints as metadata (title, location, team, workplace type, and sometimes pay). Prose is free text inside one description box, or inside Lever’s Opening / Sections / Closing boxes. The ATS does not ship headings such as “Minimum qualifications” or “Interview process.” Companies type those themselves. Apply is a form, not a prose section.

## What sections do Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever recommend or show by default?

### Takeaway

None of the three systems ships the full enterprise essay outline as fixed headings. Greenhouse’s 2026 help doc asks for a title, a description (blurb plus responsibility bullets), qualifications, and a closing company block that can hold benefits and an EEO statement. Ashby gives one Description field plus a fixed metadata rail. Lever’s editor is Opening, repeatable Sections (formerly Lists), an optional salary block, and a Closing. Everything else is employer-written prose or an application form.

### Cited Findings

**Greenhouse — writing guidance (prose, not database fields)**

- Help article “Best practices: Write an effective job description,” last updated 23 June 2026, lists five considerations: Job title; Description; Qualifications; Voice; Company Info. Description is “1) a high-level blurb that includes who the role is reporting to and what team the position is on and 2) bullet points that outline the responsibilities.” Qualifications are “a list of the hard skills.” Company Info is where most employers “conclude” with culture, “benefits, perks, an EEO / diversity statement, and awards,” and “should be consistent across all full-time job postings.” Voice is tone advice, not a heading. — [Greenhouse Support](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360003565931-Best-practices-Write-an-effective-job-description)
- The public Greenhouse template page is still dated 22 May 2019 on the page itself. It repeats those components and then names fill-in headings: an untitled opening (“[COMPANY] is looking for a [ROLE]”; “2 paragraphs max,” including reporting structure and team); **Who will love this job** (nouns); **What you’ll do** (verbs; team-specific work first); **You should have** (required qualifications first, then nice-to-haves in the same list); **Pay, perks and such** (culture, benefits, diversity invitation). It does not include About the team, Interview process, or How to apply as headings. It tells writers to keep a single call to action: finish the application. — [Greenhouse guidance](https://www.greenhouse.com/guidance/bookmark-this-template-for-job-descriptions)
- The 2019 template and the June 2026 help article agree on title, blurb, responsibilities, qualifications, and a closing company/benefits/EEO block. Only the 2019 page names the H2s “Who will love this job,” “What you’ll do,” “You should have,” and “Pay, perks and such.”

**Greenhouse — structured job and job-post fields**

- Default fields on every job: Internal job name, Department, Office, Number of openings. “Employment type is a custom job field that can be enabled by request,” so it is not on by default. — [Add a custom job field](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/202609855-Add-a-custom-job-field) (search index of the help article; page not re-fetched in full)
- Harvest create-job examples also pass requisition id and opening ids. Those are internal identifiers, not a candidate-facing “Job ID” heading. Public post URLs do carry a numeric id (the Per Scholas post below is `4716529006`). — [Create or update a job with Harvest API](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360029266072-Create-or-update-a-job-with-Harvest-API)
- External job-post form, article last updated 18 August 2026, fields: **Job name** (255-character limit); **Job board**; **Location** or locations; **Searchable locations** (up to seven, sent to third-party boards, separate from the location shown on the Greenhouse-hosted post); **Work type** (In person, hybrid, or remote; required if the post is shared to third-party boards; only one per post); **Pay transparency** fields; **Description** (rich text, images and video allowed); **Basic application information** (personal information and education); **Custom application question**; **Settings**; **Free job boards**. Status is toggled to **LIVE**. — [Create an external job post](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360020285311-Create-an-external-job-post)
- “Job post location is a required field for all job posts.” By default it is free text. Boards can restrict it to organization offices or a custom list. — [Configure structured job post locations](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360027858591-Configure-structured-job-post-locations)
- Pay transparency is rule-based. If no rule applies, the poster selects **N/A**. When a rule is selected, currency and a pay range are entered, and the help article says to remove pay that was typed into the description. A board can also store a **Description Introduction** and **Description Conclusion** that are prepended and appended to every post (company overview, benefits). — [Add pay transparency to a job post](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/10028084062491) (updated 2 March 2026); [Standardized post descriptions](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360048989471-Standardized-post-descriptions)

**Ashby — admin tabs and candidate-facing rail**

- Creating a posting uses five tabs: **Description**, **Basic Info**, **Application Form**, **Automation**, **Compensation**. Description has **Social Description** (200 characters, social shares only) and **Description** (shown on the job board **Overview** tab; links, images, and video allowed). A description is required to publish. — [Job Postings](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/job-postings) (fetched in full; search index date 25 August 2026)
- Basic Info fields: **Title** (public title can differ from the internal job title); **External Visibility** (Listed or Unlisted); **Third-Party Job Boards**; **Location**; **Additional Locations** (shown in a **Locations** section); **Location Type** (on-site, hybrid, or remote; defaults from the location, can be overridden); **Application Deadline** (optional; shown in the applicant’s time zone; can auto-unpublish).
- Candidate-facing order documented by Ashby: **Job Posting Title**; **Location**; **Employment Type** (pulled from the job **Settings** tab, not the posting); **Location Type**; **Department** (pulled from the job’s **Team** field); **Deadline to Apply** if set; **Compensation** only if display is on; **Overview** (the description); **Application** tab. — [Job Postings](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/job-postings)
- Compensation display is a setting, not a required essay. Admins choose Not Required, Require for all job postings, or Require for certain locations. Per posting: **Display on this job posting?** and **Display on job board?** If display is on, at least one valid salary range is required before publish. Components can include salary, equity, bonus, commission, and custom text. A **Compensation Tier Guide URL** can be linked. Ashby’s July 2026 product note says some employers keep the number off the public post and send it in email instead, because rules such as the EU Pay Transparency Directive still require a range before interviews. — [Compensation](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/compensation) (index date 10 July 2026); [Job Compensation token](https://www.ashbyhq.com/product-updates/compensation-substitution-tokens) (16 July 2026)
- Ashby does not name prose headings. The description is one rich-text field. Employers who want collapsible sections add their own H2s (PostHog’s handbook, which renders Ashby descriptions, says H2s become the table of contents and looks for headers such as “Who we're looking for”). That is a customer’s site, not Ashby’s default. — [PostHog jobs handbook](https://posthog.com/handbook/engineering/posthog-com/jobs) (index date 12 October 2026)

**A-to-Z checklist (what a serious single posting actually carries)**

Structured facts (ATS fields or board chrome). Not written as essays:

- Job title / Job name / Title
- Location, and on Ashby Additional Locations
- Workplace: Greenhouse **Work type** (In person, hybrid, remote); Ashby **Location Type**; Lever location type On-site / Hybrid / Remote
- Team: Greenhouse **Department** and **Office** (office is the structured location source when that mode is on); Ashby **Department** from **Team**, and **Employment Type** from job Settings; Lever **Department – Team** plus a commitment label such as Full-time or Employee
- Openings count and requisition or opening ids (Greenhouse job record; not shown as a heading on the live Per Scholas post)
- Public id only in the URL (Greenhouse integer; Ashby and Lever UUIDs)
- Employment type is default on Ashby, a named Lever commitment on live posts, and an optional Greenhouse custom field
- Pay range, currency, and interval, only when the company turns the widget on (Greenhouse pay-transparency rule, Ashby Compensation tab, Lever salary min/max)
- Application deadline (Ashby only, optional)
- Application form: Greenhouse basic info, education, and custom questions; Ashby Application tab; Lever custom questions. Greenhouse also adds Voluntary Self-Identification on the form when the employer uses it
- Distribution: Greenhouse Live and job board; Ashby Listed / Unlisted; Lever external, internal, unlisted, confidential

Prose (always written; headings are the company’s, except Lever’s three boxes):

- Opening summary, often untitled. Lever’s box is named **Opening**. Greenhouse’s standardized **Description Introduction** can hold the company blurb for every post
- About the company. Greenhouse calls the guidance **Company Info**. Live heading example: **ABOUT PER SCHOLAS**
- About the team. Not a system field. Seen as **About the Team** on indexed Lever posts
- Role summary, reporting line, and team. Greenhouse puts this in the untitled blurb. Live headings: **Who We Are Looking For**, **Role Overview**, **The role**
- Responsibilities. Greenhouse template **What you’ll do**. Live: **What You’ll Do**, **Key Responsibilities**. Lever: a Section/List the employer names
- Trait or “who will love this” list. Greenhouse template only. Easy to confuse with qualifications; the template wants nouns, not skills
- Qualifications. One combined list in the Greenhouse template (**You should have**, required then nice-to-haves). Live posts often split **Required Qualifications** / **Professional Qualifications** and **Preferred Qualifications**
- Early-success or 30/60/90 block. Not a default. Live Lever example: **Success Measures**. Lever’s impact guide uses 1/3/6 months
- Pay philosophy and benefits prose, separate from the numeric widget. Greenhouse template **Pay, perks and such**. Live: **Benefits & Perks**, **WHAT WE OFFER**
- Interview notes and how to ask a question. Not a default. Live: **Important Points**, **QUESTIONS?**
- How to apply. The **Apply** button and form. Per Scholas also prints an email under **QUESTIONS?**
- Equal opportunity, privacy, and sometimes an accommodations line. Live: **Our Commitment**, **Equal Employment Opportunity**. Accommodations language showed up on an indexed Greenhouse post (GoFundMe) as a form link, not as a default ATS heading
- Lever **Closing** / **Closing paragraph**, and Greenhouse **Description Conclusion**, for the repeated legal or culture ending

### Inferences

- “Minimum qualifications” and “Preferred qualifications” are a company convention, not a Greenhouse or Ashby field. Greenhouse’s own template keeps both inside **You should have**. Lever only supplies empty Sections, so a company can title one list Required and another Preferred.
- Interview process and a prose “How to apply” are optional extras. The systems already end the page with an application form. Shopify publishes its process on a separate candidate-guide page, not inside a job body. — [Shopify candidate guide](https://www.shopify.com/careers/candidate-guide) (index date 4 March 2026)
- Job id, requisition id, hiring manager, and posting owner are operational fields. Candidates see a title and a URL, not a “Job ID” heading, on the posts opened here.

### Gaps

- The May 2026 Lever “Creating and Managing Job Postings” article did not render. The department, team, location, and commitment controls are confirmed by the jobs-navigation article and by the live Woven header, but the exact editor label for commitment (the Woven page shows “Employee”) was not read off the help page.
- Ashby’s docs do not list a public “Job ID” or requisition field on the posting. Whether a given Ashby board prints an internal req id was not checked on a rendered posting, because the Ashby job pages fetched here returned an empty shell.
- No official page was found that names “accommodations” as a default section in Greenhouse, Ashby, or Lever. It appears when a company writes it.

## What order do serious postings use, from opening summary through equal opportunity?

### Takeaway

The repeatable order is metadata first, then company or mission, role summary, responsibilities, required qualifications, preferred qualifications, pay and benefits, a short apply or process note, then EEO. That order matches Greenhouse’s template plus what live boards add. It is not locked by Ashby. Lever’s product order is Opening, then Sections in whatever order the editor dragged, then the salary widget, then Closing.

### Cited Findings

- Greenhouse template order: untitled opening and two-paragraph role blurb; **Who will love this job**; **What you’ll do**; **You should have** (required, then nice-to-haves); **Pay, perks and such** (benefits and diversity close). The June 2026 help article puts Company Info, including the EEO statement, last. — [Greenhouse guidance](https://www.greenhouse.com/guidance/bookmark-this-template-for-job-descriptions); [Greenhouse Support](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360003565931-Best-practices-Write-an-effective-job-description)

### Inferences

- The assignment’s list (opening, company, team, responsibilities, minimum, preferred, compensation and benefits, interview process, how to apply, EEO) is the right checklist for a hand-written description. - Companies on Greenhouse often restate team, reports-to, employment status, and remote modality inside the prose even though Department, Office, Location, and Work type already exist as fields. Per Scholas did that. The public chrome still only showed title and “United States.”

## Which sections stay when pay or location is “to be discussed” rather than a number?

### Takeaway

No live posting was found that uses the phrase “to be discussed” for pay or for location. Pay can be absent or non-numeric while every other section stays. Location is a required Greenhouse field and a normal Ashby and Lever header; the closest real pattern is a country or “Remote,” not a blank. The confirmed example of pay without a number is a live Lever post that says “Competitive Salary - Based on experience” and still prints location, team, workplace, responsibilities, both qualification lists, a success block, interview notes, benefits, and EEO.

### Cited Findings

- Greenhouse location cannot be omitted: “Job post location is a required field for all job posts.” It may be free text, so a company can type a country or “Remote” instead of a city, but the field itself is mandatory. — [Configure structured job post locations](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360027858591-Configure-structured-job-post-locations)
- Greenhouse pay can be **N/A** when no pay-transparency rule applies. The description, location, work type (if used), and application form do not depend on a range. — [Add pay transparency to a job post](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/10028084062491)
- Live Greenhouse example with no salary number: [Per Scholas VP, Curriculum](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/perscholashires/jobs/4716529006). Location is still present (“United States,” plus a list of eligible states in the prose, and **Modality: Remote** inside the description). Sections that remain: company intro, fact block, who we are looking for, what you’ll do, professional and preferred qualifications, benefits and perks, questions, EEO, and the full application form including voluntary self-identification. This is not the phrase “to be discussed.” It is a posting that never states a figure.
- Ashby: compensation is shown only if **Display on this job posting?** is on. Publishing requires a description and an Open job. A salary range is required only when display is on. Title, location, employment type, location type, department, overview, and application stay either way. Admins can also leave compensation off the board and still send the range in email. — [Job Postings](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/job-postings); [Compensation](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/compensation); [Job Compensation token, 16 July 2026](https://www.ashbyhq.com/product-updates/compensation-substitution-tokens)
- Lever’s older help text says the salary section is optional and is omitted unless minimum and maximum are filled. Opening, sections, and closing are separate. — [2024 creating-and-managing article](https://lever-old.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042220512-Creating-and-managing-job-postings)
- Live Lever example, opened in full: [Woven by Toyota, Director, Global Head of Total Rewards](https://jobs.lever.co/woven-by-toyota/d569edcd-2f38-432d-92d8-ca32c1f74865). Header still has a place (Tokyo), department and team (Global Operations – Human Resources), commitment (Employee), and workplace (Hybrid). **WHAT WE OFFER** includes the bullet “Competitive Salary - Based on experience” and lists time off, insurance, housing allowance, and training. No dollar range appears in the fetched page. Sections that stay: About the company, Role Overview, Key Responsibilities, Required Qualifications, Preferred Qualifications, Success Measures, Important Points, benefits, Our Commitment / equal opportunity, and Apply. This role is rewards leadership, not curriculum. It is the cleanest public example found of a non-numeric pay line.
- A second indexed Lever pattern is the same idea inside benefits copy: capital.com’s “Competitive Salary” bullet under “What you will get in return,” with no figure in the snippet. The page was not opened in full, so a hidden range cannot be ruled out. — [capital.com posting](https://jobs.lever.co/capital/e9238068-2f95-4f7d-95b9-b586d4b5988f)

### Inferences

- If a company does not want a number, the sections that remain are the entire prose body plus the structured header minus the pay widget. Benefits prose usually stays, and it is where “competitive” or “based on experience” is written. EEO stays. The application form stays.
- Location is the field these systems do not treat as optional. “To be discussed” for a city was not observed. Broad values (country, Remote, Hybrid) are how posts avoid a street address while still filling the field.
- Do not invent a salary, a city, or an employment type from these examples. Per Scholas states full-time exempt and United States because that posting says so. Woven states Tokyo and Employee because that posting says so. Those facts belong to those employers.

### Gaps

- No posting was found whose pay or location line literally reads “to be discussed.”
- No Ashby posting with compensation hidden was opened, because Ashby job URLs did not render. The hide-pay behavior is from Ashby’s own docs, not from a captured board page.
- Whether Greenhouse still shows Work type on the public Per Scholas page is unclear: the fetched chrome showed title and “United States” only. Remote was inside the prose fact block, which may or may not mirror the Work type field.
